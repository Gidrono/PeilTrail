# Privacy Policy

PeilTrail does not collect, store, or share personal data.

The app runs entirely on your Mac. It only reads whether the Command key is held and the current mouse position, so it can draw a cursor trail. That information never leaves your computer. PeilTrail has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no network requests.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/Gidrono/PeilTrail/issues.
